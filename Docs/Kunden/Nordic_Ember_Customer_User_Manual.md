# Nordic Ember Customer User Manual

## 1. About Nordic Ember

Nordic Ember is a restaurant application that can be opened in a normal web browser or inside Telegram as a Telegram Mini App.

Customers can browse the restaurant, explore the menu, view dishes, make a reservation, view reservation information, and contact the restaurant.

## 2. Open Nordic Ember

Customers can normally open the restaurant through:

- the restaurant website;
- the Nordic Ember Telegram bot `@NordicEmberBot`;
- the Telegram Mini App entry point configured by the restaurant.

## 3. Choose Your Language

The application includes a customer language selector.

The default restaurant configuration provides:

- English
- Swedish

The restaurant may later enable Farsi or Turkish.

Customers only see the languages that the restaurant administrator has enabled.

Changing the available language list is an administrator function; customers cannot change the restaurant's configuration.

## 4. Home

The Home page introduces the restaurant and provides access to the main functions.

Use the navigation to move between:

- Home
- Menu
- Book Table
- About
- Contact

## 5. Browse the Menu

Open **Menu** to see the available dishes and menu sections.

Depending on the restaurant's current content, the menu can include:

- starters;
- main courses;
- desserts;
- drinks;
- dish images;
- descriptions;
- prices.

## 6. View a Dish

Select a dish to open its detailed information when the feature is available.

Review the dish name, description, price, and image before returning to the menu or continuing to the reservation process.

## 7. Make a Reservation

Open **Book Table**.

Enter the requested information, such as:

- full name;
- country code;
- telephone number;
- email address;
- number of guests;
- reservation date;
- reservation time;
- seating area;
- special requests.

Review the information carefully before submitting.

## 8. Reservation Confirmation

After a successful reservation, the application displays a confirmation result.

When production email confirmation is configured, the customer may also receive a reservation confirmation email.

Keep the booking reference shown by the application or included in the confirmation email.

## 9. Profile and Reservation Information

Open the customer Profile area when available to review reservation information stored for the current session/account experience.

The exact profile behavior depends on the installed application version.

## 10. Contact the Restaurant

Open **Contact** to see the restaurant's communication details.

Depending on the configuration, this can include:

- address;
- telephone;
- email;
- website;
- Telegram Concierge/contact option.

## 11. Use Nordic Ember Inside Telegram

To use the application from Telegram:

1. Search for `@NordicEmberBot`.
2. Open the bot.
3. Press the configured button such as **Open Restaurant** or the equivalent Mini App button.
4. The Nordic Ember application opens inside Telegram.
5. Use Home, Menu, Book Table, About, and Contact as normal.

The Telegram interface is the same restaurant application served by the production web application.

## 12. Mobile Use

Nordic Ember is designed to work well on mobile devices.

Inside Telegram, use normal touch gestures and the application's navigation buttons.

## 13. If a Reservation Does Not Work

Check that:

- the reservation date is valid;
- the selected time is available;
- the guest information is complete;
- the phone number is valid;
- the email address is correctly entered when required.

If the application still does not accept the reservation, contact the restaurant.

## 14. Important Privacy and Security Note

Never share another customer's reservation information.

Do not share administrator access or administrator credentials with customers.
